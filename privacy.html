Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2025

1. Introduction

DSentiment Gmail Integration ("we," "our," or "us") provides a secure Gmail API integration service that enables users to authenticate with their Gmail accounts and access their email data through our RESTful API. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our service.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Authentication Data: Your Gmail email address and OAuth tokens required for accessing your Gmail account
  • Email Data: When you use our service, we temporarily process your email messages, including sender information, subjects, snippets, and dates
  • Technical Data: API usage logs, request timestamps, and error logs for service optimization

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely for the following purposes:

  • To authenticate your identity and provide secure access to your Gmail data
  • To process and return your email data as requested through our API
  • To maintain and improve our service functionality
  • To troubleshoot technical issues and provide customer support

4. Data Storage and Security

We implement the following security measures:

  • No Email Storage: We do not permanently store your email content or metadata
  • Secure Transmission: All data transmission uses HTTPS encryption
  • Access Control: Only authorized personnel have access to our systems
  • Token Expiration: Authentication tokens have automatic expiration and refresh mechanisms

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to third parties. We may share your information only in the following circumstances:

  • With your explicit consent
  • To comply with legal obligations or court orders
  • To protect our rights, property, or safety, or that of our users

6. Data Retention

We retain your information as follows:

  • OAuth Tokens: Stored until you revoke access or tokens expire
  • Email Data: Processed in real-time and not stored permanently
  • Log Data: Retained for 30 days for troubleshooting purposes

7. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Access: Request information about the data we have about you
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your authentication tokens and associated data
  • Revocation: Revoke access to your Gmail account at any time through your Google Account settings
  • Portability: Request a copy of your data in a machine-readable format

8. Third-Party Services

Our service integrates with Google's Gmail API. Your use of Gmail is subject to Google's Privacy Policy and Terms of Service. We recommend reviewing these policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Terms of Service: https://policies.google.com/terms

9. Children's Privacy

Our service is not intended for use by children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the effective date.

11. International Users

If you are accessing our service from outside the United States, please note that your information may be transferred to and processed in the United States, where our servers are located.
